# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RepoLens is an open-source project and aims to provide a welcoming, respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and other disrespectful conduct are not welcome.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not include private credentials, secrets, or other sensitive information in public issues.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project healthy.
